Our Story

Three Generations of Baking Excellence

Founded in 1987 in a small Parisian side street, La Pâtisserie began as a family dream. Grand-mère Colette's recipes form the soul of everything we create.

Today, we honour those traditions while embracing modern artistry. Every morning at 4am, our bakers laminate dough, temper chocolate, and coax exquisite flavours from the finest ingredients.

The Craft

From Flour to Perfection

Each pastry undergoes a meticulous journey, guided by skilled hands and time-honoured techniques.

🌾 01

Source

Organic flour from heritage mills and butter from small Norman farms.

🤲 02

Prepare

Doughs mixed slowly and rested. Croissant dough laminated by hand over two days.

🔥 03

Bake

Stone-deck ovens with steam injection create the perfect crust every time.

✨ 04

Present

Final touches of glaze and garnish transform baked goods into edible art.
